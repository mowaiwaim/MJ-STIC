Implementation. At a completed all-node impulse at reference time u, sample Q0 = e(u)' M e(u) after the jump. If mode 2 persists, sample Q1 at u+delta with no jump. Equation (5) gives post-dwell extension (gamma+ln(Q0/Q1))/(lambda+lambda0). Its infimum definition gives zero extension if the numerator is nonpositive. We apply the network-specific upper bound H2 to the total proposed delay, giving d=min(H2,delta+max(0,(gamma+ln(Q0/Q1))/(lambda+lambda0))). At time zero and on entering mode 2, we use Eq. (5)'s initial convention bar(zeta_0)=zeta_0, hence the log ratio equals zero. A mode switch cancels both the probe and the pulse and resets the mode clock. The network constraint H2, the mode-specific activation and cancellation, and the scalar all-node jump factor 0.5 are our adaptations. Clock weights, delays, diffusion, Markov paths, the held-out Brownian increments and the mode-1 feedback are from Example 3. All three errors are sampled once at a reference and additionally at each completed probe.

Parameter choice. delta=0.1, lambda=2 and lambda0=0.01 follow the Zhan example. gamma is calibrated using only the number of pulses on the 256 pilot paths, seed 512091; the target is the computed pilot MJ-STIC average 16.08984375. Results in zhan_pilot.csv list every candidate and mark the chosen entry. The held-out set is 384 paths with seed 73129. Inputs are zero, the horizon is 8 and the base step is 0.005. No error outcome participates in parameter selection. Event-aligned Euler-Maruyama steps are also split at probe times to obtain the state at this additional observation. A reference and its post-jump sample are counted once. Counts for mode entry include canceled episodes.

Certificate on the common network. The original paper's Theorem 1 treats a stochastic plant without state delay or Markov mode cancellation. Our bound follows by reusing the drift and reset estimates in the current manuscript for this adapted timer. In every mode-2 episode, new probe and deadline times depend only on available samples. The elapsed clock a lies in [0,H2] until execution, cancellation, or the end of the observation horizon. A pure probe alters neither e, a, the mode, nor the Lyapunov functional. The storage weight c2 exp(-nu2 a) remains inside the certified interval. At an executed impulse the quadratic-state part of the storage is nonincreasing since (1+mu2)^2 exp(nu2 a)<=0.25 exp(2.4*0.45)=0.736169887<1. The delay integral is continuous at an impulse. The switches have the same reset and generator as in Example 3, and the same drift estimates, negative matrix margins and zero-input exponential comparison therefore apply. The argument verifies the extension to the two-observation rule. The letter's parameters gamma>lambda*delta and gamma+lambda0*delta<log(4) are also satisfied but alone do not prove the result for the delayed switched network.
